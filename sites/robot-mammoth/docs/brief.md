# Robot Mammoth: brief

Track: own. Intake on 2026-11-09, three questions.

## In Goutham's words
- Premise: "life of a 3D Robot Elephant living in Lush Green Amazon Forests of Amazon"
- Why it's there: "It is sent there to observe the wild life in their own habitat, this has to be unique"
- The one moment: "How it can hide itself in that forest, how it can adapt. If amazon forest is difficult then make it a mammoth in snowy mountains"
- Award: "All"
- Time: "1hr per day"

## Claude's calls (proposals; correct any of them)
- **World:** a robot mammoth in snowy mountains, not an elephant in the Amazon. A premium jungle can't be built in code to run well on phones and would need real forest imagery, which kie.ai being ruled out leaves without a source. Snow, fog and terrain can be built in code, and hiding reads instantly in a white-out.
- **Story angle:** the only mammoth left is a machine, sent to watch animals that are still alive.
- **Bar:** build to Awwwards' standard (usability and phone speed), keep the hiding effect ambitious enough for FWA, submit to all three at launch.
- **Time:** the 1 hour a day is Goutham's review and decision time; Claude does the build. So each check-in should end with one decision, and the scope is one long scroll page plus a short field-notes page.

## Not asked (own track)
Journey, energy curve, references, how far from minimal, one world or scenes, assets: decided in Research and Concept, and shown to Goutham as proposals.
