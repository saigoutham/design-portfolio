# Product

## Platform
web: desktop and phone, with phones art-directed separately.

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind 4, GSAP with ScrollTrigger, Lenis, React Three Fiber with drei and postprocessing. Deployed on Vercel from `sites/robot-mammoth`.

## Users
Award jurors (Awwwards, FWA, CSS Design Awards) and the design and creative-development community who browse award sites. They give a site a few minutes, on desktop and on phone. (Claude's proposal.)

## Product Purpose
A concept story site: a robot mammoth sent into snowy mountains to observe wildlife in its own habitat, and how it hides itself and adapts to do that. It's a portfolio piece for Goutham, aimed at Awwwards, FWA and CSS Design Awards.

## Positioning
An invented concept, labelled as one. Not a product, not a real research project.

## Operating Context
Own track, no client. Goutham reviews about an hour a day; Claude builds. No CMS: the content is fixed at launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- No paid image or video generation (kie.ai ruled out). Visuals are built in code (React Three Fiber, shaders, particles) or made by Goutham.
- Must stay smooth on a mid-range phone; a still image shows first, and the 3D loads after first paint.
- A reduced-motion version that keeps the composition.
- Real wildlife facts only; no invented statistics.

## Brand Commitments
- Honest about being a concept.
- Observational and quiet: the mammoth watches, it doesn't perform. (Claude's proposal.)
