# Product

## Platform
web: phone first, desktop art-directed separately. Must hold up on a mid-range Android phone on a mobile connection.

## Stack
Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind, GSAP with ScrollTrigger, Lenis. React Three Fiber only if the concept needs it (decided at stage 4). Deployed on Vercel from `sites/diwali-festival-of-lights`.

## Users
Anyone meeting Diwali's stories, of any age, including people new to the festival. First priority: a first-time visitor on a phone (Claude's proposal). Secondary: award jurors (Awwwards, FWA, CSS Design Awards).

## Product Purpose
Tell the stories behind Diwali as one immersive experience: one night, many lamps, each lamp opening one story in the art tradition of the state that tells it. For Ira, a storyteller of epics.

## Positioning
A retelling for everyone, not a religious authority and not a festival guide. Each tradition is honoured in its own visual language; the site makes clear that Diwali is many stories, not one.

## Operating Context
Client track (Ira). Launch Mon 23 Nov 2026, the eve of Dev Diwali. Quality over scope. English only. No CMS: content fixed at launch; nobody edits after launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Six stories, three scenes each, every one built from scratch: no client assets.
- Art: layered vector art drawn in code, after Madhubani, Tholu Bommalata, Pattachitra, Dashavatara Ganjifa (Sawantwadi), Jain Kalpasutra painting, and Phulkari. No paid or generated imitations of living crafts.
- Reduced-motion version that keeps the composition and every word of the stories.
- Text readable aloud to a child; plain English, original terms kept where needed.
- Cookie-free measurement of how many visitors light every lamp.

## Brand Commitments
- Accuracy: every telling traceable to published sources, checked by Ira.
- No human depiction of the Sikh Gurus. Jain and Sikh observances framed as their own lamps, not parts of a Hindu epic.
- Art credited "after <tradition>", never presented as the craft itself.
- A quiet ending: no call to action, no sign-up.
