# Art proof critic: V (c2-2)

## 1. What happens
A macro watermelon cross-section carries the cream headline "Real fruit. Real fizz."; a thin glossy waterline rises from the bottom (V-1.5), sweeps up through the type (V-2.5, V-3.5), and leaves bubble streams rising through the fruit below it (V-5).

## 2. Scores (start 6)
- **Craft: 5.** V-0.3 is the best frame: wet glints, sheen on the seeds and credible pulp grain make the fruit believable at a glance. But the flesh reads as raw meat more than juicy melon: it is over-sharpened, uniformly saturated red, and has no translucency and no pale rind or white-to-pink gradient to say "watermelon". The liquid does not exist as a material. In V-1.5 and V-5 the surface is a one-pixel white wire with a rounded corner at the left edge, and the region below it is the same image slightly more saturated. There is no volume, no meniscus thickness, no tint shift, no refraction of the fruit beneath, and no caustics. The type is legible at rest (V-0.3, cream on red, strong contrast) but loses its edges in V-2.5 and V-3.5, where the clipped and ghosted duplicate under the line looks like a compositing error. In V-5 bubbles sit on top of the letters and the head of "Real fruit." is sliced off at the waterline. Bubbles are uniform pink-ringed sprites in rigid vertical columns with no size variance, no highlights, no clustering at seeds and no foam at the surface (V-3.5, V-5). They look like stickers, not CO2.
- **Motion: 5.** The idea reads: a rising line, then bubbles. But the line moves like a mask wipe at constant tension, with no wave, no slosh, no lag and no bubble release at the surface. The type jumps left by about 45px between V-2.5 and V-3.5 with no explanation. Bubbles appear in V-2.5 and are dense by V-3.5, but the whole thing feels linear, not physical.
- **Creativity: 6.** Submerging the fruit in fizz with the headline refracting through the surface is a subject-specific idea (V-2.5, V-3.5). It earns no more than 6 because the transition is a known pattern (wipe line plus particles) and the payoff frame V-5 is less appealing than the opening frame.

## 3. Top fixes
1. **V-1.5 to V-5, liquid body.** The waterline has no liquid. Render a real volume: a pale pink-green tint, a refraction offset of the fruit below it, thick bright meniscus with soft caustics, and a slight blur and desaturation under the surface. The type below the line should refract and wobble, not duplicate.
2. **V-3.5 and V-5, bubbles.** Vary size 3x, add specular highlights and a rim, cluster them at the seeds and rind pores, vary speed, and burst them into fine foam at the surface. Break the rigid columns.
3. **V-2.5 and V-3.5, type.** Stop slicing the type with a hard line and a ghost copy. Refract it continuously through the surface, keep the x position fixed, and keep the final frame's headline clear of bubble clutter (V-5 hurts legibility). Also grade V-0.3 toward juicy pink-red with a visible light-scatter gradient, so it reads as watermelon and not meat.

A jury would call the final frame a filter over a photo. Even with layout added, the hero's signature moment does not sell a drink.

VERDICT: FAIL
