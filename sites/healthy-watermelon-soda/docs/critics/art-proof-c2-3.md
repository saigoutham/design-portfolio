# Art proof C2-3: critic read (V, hero art)

## 1. What happens
A macro watermelon cross-section with the headline "Real fruit. Real fizz." gets a thin white liquid line rising from the bottom; it sweeps up through the type, and fizz bubbles stream up behind it until the frame is bubbles over fruit.

## 2. Scores (start 6, evidence must earn more)

| Area | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | 6 | V-0.3: the photo and the heavy cream grotesque are strong and legible, but that is a good stock-style frame, not a crafted one. V-2.5: the headline doubles into a ghosted copy under the line, which reads as a glitch rather than refraction. V-1.5: the rounded corner at the left edge makes the line look like a glass-card or UI outline, not liquid. V-3.5/V-5: bubbles are near-uniform, small and pasted on, and they sit over the type and cut into legibility. Nothing earns a 7. |
| Motion | 6 | V-1.5 to V-3.5: the line sweeping upward through the frame is a directional wipe with a wobble, and the region below the line is only a brighter tint of the same photo. No seed or flesh refraction shows, so it does not read as liquid. V-3.5 to V-5: the state barely changes (line moves ~50px, bubbles thicken), so there is no climax and no resolving beat. Nothing here asks for a scroll beyond "see what's next". |
| Creativity | 6 | The idea (fruit turning into fizz, the liquid level as the scroll indicator) is subject-specific and clear. But the carry-through is a rising line plus bubbles, which is a familiar "liquid fill" and "bubbles overlay" pattern. V-5 ends on no reveal, product or line of copy, so there is no moment to retell. |

## 3. Top 3 fixes
1. V-1.5 to V-3.5, the line as a flat wipe. Make it a real meniscus: refract and magnify the flesh and seeds below it (visible seed distortion, a lensing band at the edge), and give the submerged zone a different color and depth than the dry fruit. Remove the rounded left corner so it fills edge to edge.
2. V-2.5, the headline doubling. Replace the ghost duplicate with a deliberate treatment: split "Real fruit." (dry) from "Real fizz." (submerged, refracted and lifting with the bubbles), so the type itself shows the idea.
3. V-5, no payoff. The frame needs a destination: bubbles converge into a glass, a can or a colored flood with the line resolved. Vary bubble size, depth blur and speed, and keep them from covering the headline's counters.

## 4. Verdict
Photographic quality and the type are solid and the concept is on-subject, but the motion is a wipe with a bubble overlay, with visible artifacts and no climax. It does not reach the 7.2 weighted bar for a Site of the Day hero.

VERDICT: FAIL
