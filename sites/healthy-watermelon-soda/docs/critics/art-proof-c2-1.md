# Art proof C2, critic 1: hero V

**Sequence:** soda rises over a static macro photo of watermelon flesh: a waterline climbs from below the frame (V-1.5) to the upper third (V-5), bubbles rise in columns beneath it, and the headline "Real fruit. Real fizz." distorts as the liquid passes over it.

## Scores

- **Craft: 5.** The photo carries V-0.3, but the liquid isn't believable. In V-1.5 the waterline is a thin white stroke with rounded corners at the left edge, like a UI card edge. Below the line the photo only gets a little more saturated: no darkening, no caustics, no depth. In V-3.5 the submerged type shows a misregistered double image (ghosted "Real fruit." offset to the left), which looks like a glitch, not refraction. In V-5 the seeds at the bottom look posterized and cut out.
- **Motion: 5.** The action is readable: liquid rises, fizz follows (V-1.5 to V-5). It isn't physical. The surface stays nearly flat, with no slosh or splash where it meets the type. The bubbles are identical sprites in rigid vertical columns (V-3.5), all one size, and they sit on top of the headline instead of behind or inside it (V-5). The end state has no payoff: the line just stops and cuts through the tops of the letters (V-5).
- **Creativity: 6.** "The screen fills like a glass poured over the fruit" ties to the product, but it's a known pattern; nothing a visitor would retell (V-3.5).

## Top 3 fixes

1. **V-1.5 / V-5 waterline:** replace the stroked rounded rectangle with a real liquid surface. Use a displaced, wobbling meniscus with a bright specular rim and a soft foam band, add splash where it crosses the headline, and darken and blur the fruit under the surface for depth.
2. **V-3.5 type under liquid:** remove the doubled offset copy. Refract the glyphs with one continuous displacement driven by the surface normal so the letters wobble as one shape instead of ghosting.
3. **V-3.5 / V-5 bubbles:** randomize size (2 to 18 px), speed and wobble, add depth of field and occlusion behind the type, and cluster the bubbles at the seeds as nucleation points. End on a designed beat, such as the fizz surge settling into the headline fully submerged.

## Verdict

Readable but generic, with craft faults in every late frame.

VERDICT: FAIL
