# Art proof 1: juror critique (X vs Y)

Judged from six frames in /tmp/critic-evidence only, against the jury "Score rubric".

## 1. What happens

- **X:** a whole, pale photo-textured watermelon with a seam already showing (X-0) splits into two floating halves (X-1.5) that turn their red faces toward the camera and settle (X-3).
- **Y:** a stylized, glossy CG watermelon that starts already cut in two (Y-0), and the halves drift apart and turn face-on (Y-1.5, Y-3), casting hard shadows.

## 2. Scores (start at 6; evidence earns points above it)

| | Craft | Motion | Creativity |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 6 | 5 | 4 |
| Y | 4 | 4 | 3 |

- **X Craft 6:** the flesh in X-1.5 has believable grain, seeds and a white rind band. But the cut line is visible before the cut (X-0), the rind is washed-out mint, and the flesh in X-3 reads as a flat photo decal with no wet sheen or depth. In X-1.5 the halves float over a vague pink smudge with no contact shadow.
- **X Motion 5:** an intact melon becoming open halves is a clear before and after (X-0 to X-3), but nothing causes it: no blade, no pressure, no burst, no juice. The halves just slide apart.
- **X Creativity 4:** a halved watermelon is the default stock image for this subject. Nothing in X-3 says soda, fizz or "healthy".
- **Y Craft 4:** the rind has a plastic-like specular blowout (Y-1.5, top right). The cut faces have a hard dark-green outline. The seeds are an identical, symmetric decal ring (Y-3). The flesh is a flat gradient. It reads as a game asset, not a studio render. The cast shadows (Y-3) are the one plus.
- **Y Motion 4:** it is already open at Y-0, so there is no event. From 0 to 3 s it is only a rotation.
- **Y Creativity 3:** the same idea as X, with worse execution.

## 3. Stronger: X. Three fixes

1. **X-0, giveaway seam:** remove the pre-cut groove so the whole melon looks whole. Make the split an event: a crack that propagates through the rind with a slight squash before it opens.
2. **X-1.5, nothing ties it to soda:** the split must release the product. Juice that carbonates mid-air, bubbles rising off the flesh, or the halves revealing a can or a pour inside. This is the brand idea and it is missing.
3. **X-3, flat flesh and floating halves:** give the cut face wet specular, subsurface scattering and a slight juice bead at the rind. Add contact shadows and a deeper rind green so the halves sit in a lit space rather than on a gradient.

## 4. Verdict

X is a competent, polished but generic 3D fruit cut in half. At best it supports Creativity 4 to 5 and Design 6, well under a weighted 7.2. Neither art could carry a Site of the Day hero yet.

VERDICT: FAIL
