# Art proof r2, critic 1: candidate Z

**What happens:** a whole watermelon on a pink void gets a pink cut line, splits into two halves that turn to show their faces, and pink bubbles rise from the gap between them.

## Scores

- **Craft: 5.** The textures hold up at a glance (Z-2.5), but the object is a CG egg: a perfect ellipsoid with a plastic hotspot (Z-0). The cut is a thin 2D line painted on the surface (Z-0.8). The cut faces are flat decals with no wetness, depth or juice sheen (Z-2.5, Z-3.5). The rind stripes smear and stretch on the turned halves (Z-3.5, left half). The bubbles are flat ring and dot sprites that sit on top of the flesh (Z-3.5, Z-5). No contact shadow, so the halves float (Z-2.5).
- **Motion: 6.** The action is easy to read from stills: cut, split, open, fizz (Z-0.8 to Z-5). Nothing earns more: there is no anticipation or impact at the split (Z-1.5 is just two halves apart), the halves never move apart in a way that shows weight, and by Z-5 the fizz is a uniform fountain.
- **Creativity: 5.** "Slice a watermelon and it fizzes" is the first idea anyone would have for a watermelon soda. Nothing in Z-5 would make a visitor tell a friend about it. It has no liquid, no can and no "healthy" angle.

## Top 3 fixes

1. **Z-2.5 / Z-3.5, cut faces:** these are flat photo decals. Give the flesh a displaced, granular surface with a wet specular layer, juice beading at the rind edge, and seeds with real depth. Add a short juice spray at the moment of the split.
2. **Z-3.5 / Z-5, bubbles:** the 2D ring sprites read as clip art. Render refractive bubbles that are lit, vary in size and blur with depth. Have them come out of the flesh and build to a burst.
3. **Z-5, payoff:** the sequence ends on two halves and generic fizz. It needs an end state that is about the drink, for example the fizz gathering into a can or glass silhouette, or the halves turning into liquid. Add a contact shadow and a real surface so the fruit sits somewhere.

## Verdict

A 6-level hero at best. It cannot carry a site to a weighted 7.2+.

VERDICT: FAIL
