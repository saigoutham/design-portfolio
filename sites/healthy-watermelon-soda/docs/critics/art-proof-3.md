# Art proof 3: creative director critique (X vs Y)

## 1. What I see
- X: a photo-textured whole melon with a hairline seam (X-0) splits and floats apart (X-1.5), then both halves tilt to show red cut faces side by side (X-3).
- Y: a CG melon already sliced with a thin gap (Y-0) opens, turning edge-on (Y-1.5), until the two halves face us (Y-3).

## 2. Scores (start 6)
**X**
- Craft 7: X-1.5 flesh has real grain, juice sheen and irregular seed clusters, so it reads as appetising. Held back by the flat, blotchy rind and a seam that looks pasted on (X-0), and by halves hovering over a smeared pink shadow (X-1.5).
- Motion 6: X-0 to X-1.5 to X-3 is a clear whole, split, reveal beat, but the halves move as a mirrored pair and settle into a static, slightly crooked composition (X-3). Nothing earns a scroll beyond the split.
- Creativity 5: a melon cut in half is the category cliché. No soda, no liquid, no surprise. Fails the "tell a friend" test.

**Y**
- Craft 6: contact shadows ground the halves (Y-3), rind has convincing specular. But the flesh is a flat gradient and the seeds look stuck on, so it reads as plastic CG (Y-1.5, Y-3).
- Motion 6: the edge-on slice at Y-1.5 is the best single frame in either set, but the sequence is the same open-and-turn.
- Creativity 5: same cliché, even more stock-3D-asset.

## 3. Stronger: X
Photographic flesh makes it feel like real fruit (X-1.5), and Y looks like a stock 3D model. But it is the better of two clichés, not a strong piece.

Fixes, most valuable first:
1. X-1.5, X-3 (the idea): the cut only reveals a melon. Make the reveal pay off with soda. Pour, burst or fizz from the flesh, or have the flesh become the liquid. This is the missing concept and the biggest Creativity gain.
2. X-3: composition is static and slightly crooked, with halves at different angles and no motion left. Add continuing motion (slow orbit, droplets, seed drift) and settle on a deliberate hero pose with room for type.
3. X-1.5: halves levitate over a faint pink smudge and the shadow does not follow the lift. Add a contact shadow that stretches and softens with height, plus a wet rim highlight. Also bevel the X-0 seam.

## 4. Verdict
Competent product spin of a cut fruit, with no idea a visitor would repeat and no motion that earns a scroll. An SOTD contender (7.2+) needs a signature moment, and this has none.

VERDICT: FAIL
