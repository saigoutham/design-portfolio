# Research: drinks, fruit and playful consumer sites

Three references, the category's clichés, and five unusual angles. Award claims are only what a fetched page states. Anything else is marked unconfirmed. I read case-study and listing pages, not Awwwards' own winner archive, so the More Nutrition date is as its case study states it.

## 2. What the category looks like (clichés to avoid)

- **The scroll-driven bottle.** A bottle or can spins, rotates or draws on scroll. Search returns it again and again, for example [3D product scroll animation, Bunta beer](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/3d-product-scroll-animation-bunta-beer) and [bottle roll animation, Fernet-Branca](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/bottle-roll-animation-fernet-branca-us).
- **Colour as the whole concept.** "Bold, colourful, animated" with no structural idea (Johnny's).
- **Pitch copy.** "Fun", "refreshing", "the taste you deserve".
- **The house template.** The uniqueness reference records four builds from one skeleton: full-bleed scrub hero, corner headline, pinned type act, card rail with tilt, magnet CTA, one accent. A drinks page drifts there unless its grammar is chosen on purpose.

## 3. Five unusual angles

1. **Catalog grammar, not filmic.** Each flavour is a labelled object on one fixed schema, with fact labels and no persuasion (uniqueness.md 2.6). Example: "Watermelon. Whole fruit. No added sugar. Contains naturally occurring sugars." That uses the brief's own claim wording, and drinks pages rarely do.
2. **A running receipt of what went in.** A fixed panel adds one line each time the visitor passes a claim (uniqueness.md, "running receipt"). Lines are ingredients and process steps, not figures, since the brief forbids invented numbers. It closes as a totalled ingredient list.
3. **"See how it was made" as a trace rail.** The primary action is the case study. A thin bottom rail, driven by scroll as playhead, stamps each making step (cut, press, add water, bottle) and stays as a record (uniqueness.md, "scroll-as-playhead over a persistent trace rail"). It also works as navigation.
4. **One ripeness control regrades the page.** One handle moves the whole palette from pale rind to full red, affecting every surface at once (uniqueness.md, "one control that regrades the whole page"). It is visual only, with no health claim attached.
5. **The soda narrates its own making.** Borrowed from La Revoltosa's voice idea: short first-person lines describe each step, and the case-study link is the second beat. Copy stays inside the brand rules.

Also from hero-depth.md: layers at different rates. Rind, flesh, glass and bubbles can be those planes, with a shared anchor so the glass sits on the surface. Keep body text at 4.5:1 over the flesh colour (review.md).
