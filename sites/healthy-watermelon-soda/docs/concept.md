# Concept: The screen sweats

**One line.** The site is ice cold: it opens fogged over a real watermelon, and you wipe the glass to read it.

**What a visitor tells a friend.** "The website fogged up and I had to wipe it to read."

## The signature moment, beat by beat
1. **Fogged (0 s).** Full-bleed: the owner's cut-face photo behind a sheet of condensation. Everything is soft and pale; tiny beads catch the light; a faint frost at the corners. One hint: "Wipe me."
2. **Wipe.** Finger or cursor drags clear trails. Behind them the fruit is sharp, wet and saturated; the headline "Real fruit. Real fizz." is under the glass, readable only where wiped. Where a trail passes, beads gather at its edge and some run down, leaving clear streaks.
3. **Creep back.** Wiped areas fog over again in a few seconds, from the edges inward, so the visitor keeps wiping. Keyboard: arrow keys wipe a band; reduced motion: no fog, the photo and type plain.
4. **Colder (scroll).** Each section is colder: more beads, larger drops, a frost edge growing in from the corners. The content sits under the glass: "No added sugar. Contains naturally occurring sugars." "Watermelon, water, fizz." "How it was made."
5. **The end.** Fully frosted. One wipe reveals "Drink it before it warms up." and the primary action, "See how it was made".

## Sections
Hero (fogged fruit, wipe) · What's in it (three ingredients under the glass) · How it was made (the case study rail) · Close (frost, last wipe, CTA).

## Copy angle
Short, cold, physical: words a person says holding a cold can. Labelled as a concept brand. No numbers, no "sugar-free".

## Art
Code first: a condensation shader (fog density field in a render target updated by the wipe, droplets as refractive beads that run along gravity, a frost edge), over the owner's stills (cut-face, whole, glass) as the photographic layer. The photos already pass critics on craft; the shader is the idea.
