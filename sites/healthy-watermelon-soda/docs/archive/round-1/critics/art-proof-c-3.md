# Art proof W, critic 3 (self-score from evidence only)

**What happens:** a macro watermelon cut with the headline "Real fruit. Real fizz." is flooded from the bottom by a rising, refracting pink liquid line that crosses the type and ends with the frame submerged and bubbling.

## Scores (start 6)

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | 6 | W-0.3: strong poster frame; wet macro photo, clean cream grotesk, though the face is a safe default. W-2.5: the waterline is a fine detail, with a thin highlight and letters wobbling as they cross it. W-3.5 and W-5: the submerged half is a flat pink wash that loses depth, the headline goes pale pink on pink (low contrast), and bubbles are uniform, small and sticker-like, some sitting on the type. |
| Motion | 6 | W-1.5 to W-3.5: one rising level. The refraction at the line (W-2.5) lifts it above a plain wipe, but it is still one gesture. No secondary motion visible (meniscus, turbulence, parallax). Bubbles only arrive after the flood (W-2.5, W-5). |
| Creativity | 6 | The fruit becoming the drink, with "fruit" above the line and "fizz" below, is tellable. But a liquid level rising over an image is a known trope that fits any beverage, and the soda state is only a tint. |

## Top 3 fixes

1. **W-3.5 / W-5, end state:** the submerged world is flat and the headline dies into it. Keep red saturation under the surface, add caustics and magnified seeds, and hold the type at full contrast so the final frame works as the hero.
2. **W-1.5 to W-2.5, motion:** make the level react: a meniscus climbing the letterforms, per-letter refraction lag, and bubbles triggered by the seeds as the line passes, so fizz is caused by the fruit.
3. **W-5, payoff:** the bubbles are generic. Release them from the seed cavities, vary size and speed by depth, and let some break the surface back into the dry half, so the two states connect.

## Verdict

The poster frame (W-0.3) is strong, but the sequence is a refractive fill-wipe, and its payoff frames (W-3.5, W-5) lose contrast and richness. It is competent at about a 6 and lacks the signature moment a 7.2+ hero needs.

VERDICT: FAIL
