# Art proof 2: X vs Y (fruit-believability lens)

## What I see
- X: a matte egg-shaped melon with a visible seam (X-0) pops into two floating halves with photographic red flesh (X-1.5), which tilt and drift (X-3).
- Y: a glossy melon already cut, with a thin slab of flesh showing (Y-0); the halves swing open (Y-1.5) and settle side by side (Y-3).

## Scores (start 6)
| | Craft | Motion | Creativity |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 6 | 6 | 5 |
| Y | 5 | 5 | 4 |

**X Craft 6.** X-1.5 and X-3: the flesh is photo-grade, with granular wet texture, red falloff, veining and seeds in believable rows; it is the only drinkable-looking surface in either set. Held down by X-0: the rind is flat and matte, the seam reads as a crack, and the halves float with no contact shadow. Both cut faces are the same texture, mirrored.
**X Motion 6.** X-0 to X-1.5 is a real reveal (whole, then split), but the stills show a pop and a drift, not choreography.
**X Creativity 5.** The cutting reveal is a known trope, and nothing says soda.

**Y Craft 5.** Y-0 and Y-3: the rind has good specular and a hard grounded shadow, but the flesh is a smooth CG gradient with a moire-like pattern. The seeds are sparse, evenly spaced and ring-arranged, and a dark outline traces the cut edge. No juice, no wetness. It reads as a stock 3D asset.
**Y Motion 5.** Y-0 is already split, so there is no reveal; Y-1.5 to Y-3 is a plain swing-open.
**Y Creativity 4.** The most generic version of the idea.

## Stronger: X
Appetite lives in the cut face, and X wins it clearly. Y wins on lighting, grounding and rind gloss.

## Top 3 fixes for X
1. **X-1.5, X-3 (grounding):** the halves float. Add a surface with a tight contact shadow and bounce light, and vary the two faces (different seed layout, rotated texture).
2. **X-0 (rind):** matte, with a crack-like seam. Add clearcoat and waxy bloom with broken highlights (Y-3 shows the level), and turn the seam into a clean knife line with a juice bead.
3. **X-1.5, X-3 (juice):** the flesh is photographic but dry-lit. Add wet specular on the cut face, juice running over the edge, and droplets or mist at the split. This also ties the art to a drink.

Even the stronger art has Craft 6, Creativity 5 and a generic split-melon idea with no soda signature. It cannot carry a 7.2+ hero.

VERDICT: FAIL
