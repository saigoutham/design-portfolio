# Art proof critique: Z (round 2, #3)

**1. Sequence.** A photoreal striped watermelon sits still, a thin pink seam appears (0.8s), the egg-shaped melon splits and the halves part to reveal red flesh (1.5s), then settle (2.5s) as a fizz of pink bubbles rises from the gap and floods upward (3.5s, 5s).

## Scores (start at 6)

**Craft: 7.** Z-2.5 and Z-3.5: the flesh texture, seeds and pale rind ring are convincing and the soft pink floor shadow grounds both halves. Z-0: rind texture is strong, specular is believable. Held back: the melon is an elongated egg, not a watermelon silhouette (Z-0), and the bubbles in Z-3.5 and Z-5 are flat vector rings pasted over photoreal flesh, so the two fidelity levels clash.

**Motion: 6.** The beat order is clear (seam, split, part, fizz; Z-0.8 to Z-5), and the seam tell at Z-0.8 is a good anticipation. But the split at Z-1.5 is a mechanical slide with no weight, no juice, no rind snap. The bubble plume (Z-5) is the only secondary action and rises from nothing.

**Creativity: 5.** Fruit cracks open, juice fizzes: this is the standard "product reveal" cliché in soda ads. Z-5 is symmetrical, centered and tidy, with nothing a visitor would retell. The melon-to-soda idea (fruit becomes carbonation) is the only specific hook, and it is stated only by pink dots. Nothing in any frame shows a can, glass or pour, so the brand idea is not carried.

## Top 3 fixes

1. **Z-3.5 / Z-5, bubbles:** flat rings and a vertical column over a still scene. Make the flesh itself fizz: seeds and juice lift off the cut face, with bubbles refracting the flesh color and varying in depth and blur.
2. **Z-1.5, split:** a clean slide-apart with no consequence. Add rind-crack lead-in, a juice burst, a tilt and settle with weight, and a camera push so the reveal feels like an event.
3. **Z-0 to Z-5, idea:** nothing transforms into soda. Let the bubbles or juice gather into the product (a poured stream, a can silhouette, or a liquid shape) so a visitor has something to tell a friend, instead of a generic cut-open melon.

## Verdict

Competent, polished, but a known cliché with a clash between photoreal fruit and flat bubbles. Not Site of the Day hero art.

VERDICT: FAIL
