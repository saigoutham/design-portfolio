# Art proof W, critic C-1

**Sequence:** A static macro of watermelon flesh under a white "Real fruit. Real fizz." headline is slowly flooded from the bottom by a pink liquid layer whose bubbles thicken until the headline sits submerged and washed out.

## Scores
- **Craft 6.** W-0.3: the fruit macro is rich (wet speckle, glossy seeds) and works as a poster. But W-3.5/W-5 show the liquid as a flat, milky pink overlay with a hairline white meniscus: no thickness, no refraction shift or magnification of the flesh below, no caustics. It lowers contrast and makes the fruit look desaturated rather than seen through soda. The type is a default neutral grotesk with no relationship to the material.
- **Motion 6.** The action is easy to read: liquid rises and fizz grows (W-1.5 to W-5). But W-0.3 to W-1.5 are nearly identical, so the first stretch of scroll is wasted. The surface stays almost flat and rises at an even, linear-feeling rate. In W-5 the bubbles are evenly scattered sprites with no streams, depth blur or popping at the surface.
- **Creativity 6.** "The screen fills with soda over real fruit" is a known liquid-fill pattern. Drowning the line "Real fizz" in fizz is a small, literal tie to the copy (W-3.5), not something a visitor retells.

## Top 3 fixes
1. **W-3.5 / W-5: the liquid has no body.** Replace the tint with a refractive volume. Offset and magnify the fruit below the surface, give the meniscus a bright lip and dark band, add moving caustics, and keep the soda clear and sparkling instead of milky.
2. **W-2.5 to W-5: the bubbles are uniform pink sprites.** Have bubbles nucleate from the seeds in rising streams, sized and blurred by depth, popping and spitting at the surface. "Real fruit makes real fizz" then becomes the idea itself, not wallpaper.
3. **W-0.3 / W-5: no start beat and no payoff.** Start the motion immediately. End on a climax: the surface crests over the headline and the type breaks through the surface, or re-forms crisp above the foam. Don't stop on a half-flooded, washed-out frame.

## Verdict
The fruit photo carries one strong still. The soda, which is the product and the motion, reads as a colour overlay. Once layout is added, this would land around 6.5 weighted, short of 7.2.

VERDICT: FAIL
