# Brief: Healthy Watermelon Soda

Own track. Interviewed 2026-11-09. Answers are verbatim; anything marked *(studio call)* was filled in by the studio and can be overruled.

## The product
> "It's a brand that i plan to make, there are no healthy sodas available but I want to venture into this segment without any sugar, real fruit and make it fun with the soda"

Studio notes, from research on 2026-11-09:
- The category isn't empty. Misfits and DRIX (both Bangalore), plus Bubz, Hadō and Chini Kum, already sell low- or zero-sugar sodas in India. The site must not claim "first" or "only".
- Real fruit means natural sugar. FSSAI's "sugar-free" claim allows at most 0.5 g of total sugars per 100 ml, natural sugars included. The site's claim is **"no added sugar"**, shown with "contains naturally occurring sugars". No nutrition numbers until a real formulation exists.

## Purpose
> "It's just for portfolio so that should be okay"

- An invented brand, presented as a concept: the site says so plainly (honesty rule 9).
- Primary action *(studio call)*: "see how it was made" (the case study), with "work with me" as the second action.

## The one moment
> First answer: "How beautiful, unique and crazy immersive experience that the website has given. Its a totally new and fun take to present the product."

Too vague to build on; the studio offered three concrete versions and the user picked **A**:
> Scrolling slices a real 3D watermelon open; the red flesh starts to fizz and the seeds rise as bubbles. What a visitor tells a friend: "I cut a watermelon by scrolling and it turned into soda."

## Award
> "Awwwards"

Awwwards Site of the Day first. Usability is 30% of the score, so the slice must run smoothly on a mid-range phone.

## Time
> "2 hrs per day and the delivery in a week"

Target: live by **2026-11-16**. About 14 hours of the user's time, spent on decisions and reviews; the studio does the making.

Scope that follows *(studio call)*: one continuous scene (slice → fizz → product → a short "how it was made" → close). No second act, no CMS, no shop.

## Vibe, references, assets
- Vibe *(studio call)*: summer-bright, playful, tactile. Fun comes from the fruit's physical behaviour, not from cartoon graphics.
- Assets in hand: none. The watermelon is a Blender model with photo-based textures; the can or bottle is a Blender render. Nothing is drawn in code (floor rule 2).
- References: chosen at stage 2.
