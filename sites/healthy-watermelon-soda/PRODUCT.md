# PRODUCT: Healthy Watermelon Soda (concept)

## Platform
Web, one long page. Desktop and phone carry equal weight; a mid-range Android is the performance baseline.

## Stack
Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, React Three Fiber + drei, Blender (bpy) for models and renders. Hosted on Vercel.

## Users
Awwwards jurors and design-literate visitors first; potential clients and employers looking at Goutham's portfolio second.

## Product Purpose
A portfolio concept that shows an invented soda brand through one signature interaction: scrolling slices a real-looking watermelon, which turns into soda. Primary action: see how it was made (the case study). Secondary action: get in touch.

## Positioning
Real watermelon, no added sugar, actually fun. Not "the first healthy soda": that claim is false (Misfits, DRIX, Bubz, Hadō and Chini Kum exist).

## Operating Context
A solo build in one week (live by 2026-11-16). The owner has about 2 hours a day for decisions and reviews. No CMS, no shop, no editing after launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The slice works by scroll, touch and keyboard, and has a reduced-motion version.
- Weight budget: hero under about 3 MB fully loaded; LCP under 2.5 s on a throttled phone.
- Art comes from Blender models and photo textures, never from SVG or CSS drawings.

## Brand Commitments
- Labelled as a concept brand on the page.
- "No added sugar" plus "contains naturally occurring sugars"; never "sugar-free" or "zero sugar".
- No invented nutrition numbers, reviews, testimonials or statistics.
- No vendor or tool mentions in the site's copy.
