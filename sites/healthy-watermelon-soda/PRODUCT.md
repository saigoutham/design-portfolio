# PRODUCT: Healthy Watermelon Soda (concept)

## Users
Awwwards jurors and design-literate visitors first; potential clients and employers looking at Goutham's portfolio second.

## Purpose and primary action
A portfolio concept for an invented watermelon soda: real fruit, no added sugar, fun. One immersive scroll experience that makes the product feel real and playful. Primary action: see how it was made (the case study). Secondary: work with me.

## Positioning
Real watermelon, no added sugar, actually fun. Never "first", "only", "sugar-free" or "zero sugar".

## Platform and stack
One long page, desktop and phone equal; a mid-range Android is the performance baseline. Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, React Three Fiber; HyperFrames for the animatic. Vercel.

## Constraints
- Art: code first; the owner's AI-generated stills as surfaces on coded 3D. Labelled AI-generated in the case study.
- Hero under about 3 MB; LCP under 2.5 s on a throttled phone; works by scroll, touch and keyboard; a reduced-motion version.
- 2 hours a day of the owner's time; animatic first, site live by 2026-11-24.

## Brand commitments
Labelled as a concept. "No added sugar" with "contains naturally occurring sugars". No invented numbers, reviews or testimonials. No vendor mentions in the copy.
