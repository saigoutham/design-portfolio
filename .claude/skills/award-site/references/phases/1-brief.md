# Phase 1: brief

One message from you, one reply from the user. Not an interview across five turns.

1. Before asking anything, spend one or two web searches on the product's category so your questions and defaults are informed, and so you can correct a wrong premise (a "first of its kind" claim, a label claim the law doesn't allow).
2. Send **one message** with at most five questions. Each has your recommended default, so the user can answer "all defaults" or change one:
   - What it is and whether it's real (real brands need real claims; invented ones get a concept label).
   - What a visitor should do before leaving (the one primary action).
   - The moment they'd describe to a friend. Offer three concrete on-screen moments, each one makeable from an open art rung (`system/capabilities.json`), with your pick.
   - Which award first.
   - Time and deadline.
   Also say which art rungs are open and what that rules out, and ask for any art they already have. If the probe says no rung can make hero art on its own, the art question is not optional: name the exact files to send (one shoot series of stills, 2-3 free clips) or the key to set.
3. If the user said "use your judgment", skip the questions: write the brief yourself, starting `Self-authored under creative delegation`.
4. Write `docs/brief.md` (their words quoted, your calls marked "studio call") and `PRODUCT.md` (platform, users, purpose, positioning, primary action, constraints, brand commitments).
5. Client track: send the brief as a doc and wait for `gate 1`.

Scope follows time: under two weeks means one continuous scene and no CMS.
