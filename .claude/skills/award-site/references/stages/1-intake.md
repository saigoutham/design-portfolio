# Stage 1: Intake

Goal: the client's (or user's) intent in their own words, plus product truth in a format every later skill can read.

Run **brainstorming**, one question at a time, in **grilling**'s style: push on vague answers, ask for examples and numbers, and don't accept "make it memorable". Brainstorming normally hands straight to writing-plans; here that hand-off waits until stage 6, once concept, direction and assets exist.

## Question bank (skip anything already answered)

From scroll-craft's brief (`library/scroll-craft/SKILL.md`, Step 0):
- Three to five words for the vibe, plus up to three references from any medium: a film, an album, a shop. Never "sites you like"; that's how a page ends up resembling an existing site.
- The journey, section by section, in their words.
- Where it should feel calm and where intense.
- How a visitor should feel stage by stage, and the one moment they'll remember.
- One thing this site should do that no site they've seen does. This seeds the signature move, so push until it's concrete.
- How far from premium-minimal: brutalist, maximalist, playful, retro, dense, editorial.
- One continuous world, or distinct scenes.
- What assets they already have: photos, footage, brand kit, product shots.

From elaya's landing-page intake (`library/elaya/landing-page-design/SKILL.md`, A1):
- The one primary action, and the offer exactly.
- Who it's for, and the top three objections.
- Proof: numbers, testimonials, logos. Real ones only.
- Where traffic comes from.

Operations:
- Who edits the site after launch, and how often.
- The number that says it worked after 90 days.

Client track: about 12 questions. Own track: 3 (the one moment, which award first, how much time), and if the user delegates ("use your judgment"), write the rest yourself.

## Write
- `docs/brief.md`: answers verbatim, not paraphrased into marketing copy. A delegated brief starts with `Self-authored under creative delegation`.
- `PRODUCT.md` (site root), in impeccable's sections: Platform, Stack, Users, Product Purpose, Positioning, Operating Context, Capabilities and Constraints, Brand Commitments. impeccable reads it later instead of running its own interview.
- Run **find-skills** against PRODUCT.md for anything no installed skill covers (an e-commerce platform, a booking system). Install nothing without the user's yes.
- Client track: send the brief as a doc (or `docs/brief.md` if no docs tool) for **Gate 1**. Log the approval when it comes.
- Log `stage 1 intake · done` in docs/decisions.md.

## Handoff
- Next: stage 2, Research (installs the `research` pack on first use).
- Usual recommendations: if "the thing no site does" answer is weak, say so and propose two sharper versions before research starts; if the client has no usable photos, flag the shoot now, because it's the longest lead time in the whole pipeline.
- Needs from you: Gate 1 approval (client track).
