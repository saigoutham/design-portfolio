# Stage 3: Concept

Goal: `docs/concept.md` (template in `references/templates.md`). This stage decides most of the Creativity score.

Read `library/scroll-craft/references/feel.md` and `library/scroll-craft/references/uniqueness.md` first. Then have **brainstorming** lay out 2 or 3 concepts, and write the chosen one:

1. **Journey:** 4 to 7 beats, each a shift in what the visitor knows or feels. A section that serves no beat gets cut.
2. **Grammar:** one of scroll-craft's 8 (filmic one-shot, chaptered editorial, live surface, continuous world, typographic poster, gallery/catalog, split stage, rhythmic cutlist), with a line on why each of the other 7 lost. Each grammar forbids things the others need; honour its bans.
3. **Signature move:** one bespoke interaction that exists on this site alone, seeded by the brief's "thing no site does". A recoloured recipe or retuned effect doesn't count. `library/motionsites/references/techniques/` and scroll-craft's `references/devices.md` are raw material, not answers.
4. **Feeling curve:** one line per beat, the emotion and what on screen causes it. No two adjacent beats share a feeling. One peak, with the most scroll room and a quieter beat before it. An ending that resolves instead of fading into a footer.
5. **Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where ___", filled with an experience, not a technique. The jury's reading gets checked against it.
6. **Fingerprint gate:** compare the plan with every row in `system/FINGERPRINTS.md` (not scroll-craft's own workspace registry) on grammar, navigation, hero, act shape, close and signature move. It must differ from each row on at least 4. If it fails, change the plan, never the log; a log edited to fit stops protecting anything.

Client track: the concept goes into the Gate 2 proposal, not to the client on its own; they judge it with the working hero in stage 4.

Log `stage 3 concept · done`.
