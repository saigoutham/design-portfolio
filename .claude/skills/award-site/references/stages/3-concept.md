# Stage 3: Concept

**Goal:** `docs/concept.md` (template in `references/templates.md`). This stage decides most of the Creativity score.

## Steps
Read `library/scroll-craft/references/feel.md` and `library/scroll-craft/references/uniqueness.md` first. Then have **brainstorming** lay out 2 or 3 concepts, and write up the chosen one:

1. **Journey:** 4 to 7 beats, each a shift in what the visitor knows or feels. A section that serves no beat gets cut.
2. **Grammar:** pick one of scroll-craft's 8 (filmic one-shot, chaptered editorial, live surface, continuous world, typographic poster, gallery/catalog, split stage, rhythmic cutlist), with a line on why each of the other 7 lost. Each grammar forbids things the others need, so honour its bans.
3. **Signature move:** one bespoke interaction that exists on this site alone, seeded by the brief's "thing no site does". A recoloured recipe or a retuned effect doesn't count. `library/motionsites/references/techniques/` and scroll-craft's `references/devices.md` are raw material, not answers.
4. **Feeling curve:** one line per beat, giving the emotion and what on screen causes it. Shape rules:
   - No two adjacent beats share a feeling.
   - There's one peak: it gets the most scroll room, with a quieter beat before it.
   - The ending resolves instead of fading into a footer.
5. **Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where ___", filled with an experience, not a technique. The jury's reading gets checked against it.
6. **Media the concept needs:** list the art, footage or models it depends on, and where each could come from. A concept whose look depends on art nobody can supply isn't ready (SKILL.md, quality floor rules 2 and 5).
7. **Fingerprint gate:** compare the plan with every row in `system/FINGERPRINTS.md` (not scroll-craft's own registry) on six dimensions: grammar, navigation, hero, act shape, close, signature move. It must differ from each row on at least 4. If it fails, change the plan, never the log; a log edited to fit stops protecting anything.

If two concepts are still close after brainstorming, and picking wrong would be costly, convene the **council** with `--triad design` (SKILL.md, "The council"). Otherwise choose, and say why.

On the client track, the concept goes into the Gate 2 proposal, not to the client on its own; they judge it alongside the working hero in stage 4.

## Verify
concept.md has all seven parts, and the fingerprint table shows 4 or more differences against every row.

## Log
`stage 3 concept · done`, plus a skill line for brainstorming (and the council, if it was convened).

## Handoff
- Next: stage 4, Direction and hero proof. Add `--with 3d` first if the world is real 3D.
- Usual recommendations: say which grammar won and name the strongest runner-up. If the signature move needs something risky (a 3D model, licensed art, live data), name the risk and the fallback now.
- Needs from you: own track, a yes on the concept; client track, nothing yet.
